Knewly is a local-first flashcard application offered by Midnight Monstera, LLC ("Midnight Monstera," "we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information Knewly handles, where it is stored, when limited information leaves your device, and the choices available to you.

The most important point is that your flashcard study content is stored locally by default. You do not need a Knewly account or provide identity or profile details to create, edit, or study flashcards. Knewly does not upload your deck names, card text, card images, pasted study material, or study progress to Midnight Monstera.

Knewly does not ask for or maintain a user profile containing your name, postal address, telephone number, birth date, login credentials, or similar direct identity details. This does not mean that no information capable of being treated as personal information is ever processed: if you contact support, your email address and message are received, and optional Premium or network services may process purchase tokens, subscription records, request identifiers, integrity information, and ordinary connection data such as an IP address. Those limited cases are described below.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the Knewly mobile applications and Web application, Knewly Premium purchase and entitlement services, and support communications sent to Midnight Monstera.

Use of Knewly is also subject to the Knewly Terms of Service.

Midnight Monstera, LLC is a Colorado limited liability company.

For clarity, references in this policy to "personal information" or "personal data" include information that applicable law may associate with a person, household, device, or purchase. That category can be broader than information that directly reveals a person's civil identity.

It does not govern a third-party service you choose to use, such as an app store, email provider, browser, operating-system backup service, file-sharing destination, or cloud-storage provider. Those services handle information under their own terms and privacy policies.

2. Information Knewly Handles Locally

Knewly stores the following information on your device or, for the Web application, in your browser's site storage:

  • Study content: deck names, card text, card order, card images, learning state such as Known or Needs Study, and creation or modification timestamps.
  • Draft and import content: bulk-paste text, draft cards, and files you select for import while Knewly processes them.
  • App preferences: appearance choices, saved study options, and limited Quick Tips metadata such as launch counts, the next tip threshold, and which tips have been shown.
  • Local Premium state: a limited entitlement cache used to preserve appropriate access when the store or network is temporarily unavailable. On iOS, StoreKit remains the authority for Premium access. On Android, the cache contains a signed entitlement result from Knewly's verification service.
  • Local technical diagnostics: if local storage cannot start correctly, Knewly may generate a sanitized diagnostic report containing technical details such as the platform, storage availability, schema version, error category, timing, and a random startup-attempt identifier. The report is shown to you and is not automatically sent to Midnight Monstera. If you copy or send it, you control where it goes.

None of these local features requires Knewly to receive your name, address, telephone number, birth date, account credentials, or a Knewly user identifier. Study content is not combined with a remote identity profile because Knewly does not create such a profile.

Knewly uses app-owned storage, such as a local SQLite/Drift database, app-support files, browser IndexedDB, browser local storage, and, for offline use, the browser's Cache Storage for Knewly's own app files. Knewly does not use these technologies for cross-site tracking or advertising.

The Web application loads its code and fonts only from Knewly's own website and does not contact third-party services.

Knewly does not set cookies. The browser storage used by the Web application is necessary to provide the local features you request, such as saving decks, cards, images, preferences, and other local feature state in your browser.

If you enable device backup, your operating system may include Knewly's local data in a device backup, such as Android Backup or iCloud Backup. Google, Apple, or another backup provider handles that backup under its own terms and privacy policy. Midnight Monstera cannot access, inspect, or restore an operating-system backup. Operating-system backup is not guaranteed and may be affected by device settings, storage limits, network availability, and provider policies.

Knewly does not provide cloud synchronization or a Knewly-operated automatic backup service. You are responsible for creating manual backup files, saving them in a location you trust, and maintaining any device-backup settings you choose to use. Midnight Monstera cannot recover study content that remains only on a lost, damaged, reset, or inaccessible device or in cleared browser storage.

Local information remains until you delete it in Knewly, clear Knewly's browser data, or uninstall the app, subject to the behavior of your device, browser, operating-system backups, and any backups or exports you created. Because Midnight Monstera does not receive this local information, we cannot retrieve, restore, correct, or delete it for you.

3. Camera, Photos, Files, Clipboard, Backups, and Sharing

Knewly accesses these resources only when you use the related feature:

  • Camera: if you choose to take a photo for a flashcard, Knewly requests camera access. Knewly does not request microphone access for this feature.
  • Photos and images: if you choose an existing image, Knewly asks the operating system or browser to let you select it. Knewly requests the image without its full metadata. Before saving, Knewly removes embedded metadata such as location and camera details, so stored images, exports, and backups do not carry that information.
  • Files: if you import a deck, text file, or backup, Knewly reads the file you select so it can validate and import it locally.
  • Clipboard: Knewly processes text you paste into its fields. It does not continuously monitor your clipboard. If you choose to copy a diagnostic report, Knewly writes that report to the clipboard.
  • Exports and backups: Knewly can create deck exports and full application backups containing the study information you selected, including card images in supported backup formats. Knewly then hands the file to your operating system, browser download flow, or share sheet. After you choose a destination, the recipient, storage provider, messaging app, or other destination handles the file under its own privacy practices.

You are responsible for protecting exported files. Anyone who can access an unencrypted export or backup may be able to read its contents.

4. Information Processed for Knewly Premium

Knewly's free local features do not require an account, identity profile, or Knewly server. If you view, purchase, restore, or use Knewly Premium, the applicable app store and limited entitlement systems process purchase information. Although Knewly does not receive your payment-card details or require your name for this process, purchase tokens and related records may qualify as personal information because they are persistent identifiers associated with a subscription.

Knewly Premium is available only in the iOS and Android applications. The Web application has no purchase features and does not contact Knewly's entitlement service.

Android and Google Play

Google Play processes the purchase and provides Knewly with information such as the Premium product identifier, offer or base-plan information, localized price, purchase status, and a purchase token. Knewly does not receive your payment-card number.

To verify an Android subscription, Knewly sends the following to Knewly's entitlement service over HTTPS:

  • the Google Play purchase token;
  • a randomly generated request identifier;
  • a Google Play Integrity token bound to that request; and
  • protocol and operation information needed to validate the request.

Verification happens when you purchase or restore Premium. It also happens automatically when Knewly starts or refreshes Premium status on a device where Google Play reports a Knewly Premium purchase.

Except for authorized Reviewer Access, Knewly contacts its Android entitlement service only after Google Play reports a Knewly Premium purchase. If Google Play reports no Premium purchase, no request is sent to Knewly's entitlement service.

The service uses Google Play Integrity and Google Play Developer APIs to confirm that the request comes from a recognized Knewly installation and that the subscription is valid. Google may return app-recognition, licensing, and device-integrity results and authoritative subscription information. Google Play may also send real-time subscription and voided-purchase notifications through Google Cloud Pub/Sub.

To prevent abuse, the entitlement service uses your IP address transiently to rate-limit verification and reviewer-access requests. Knewly does not store IP addresses in its entitlement database.

Authorized Google Play reviewers can optionally enter a review code to request seven days of Premium access without a purchase or Knewly account. This sends the code, a random request identifier, protocol information, and a request-bound Play Integrity token to the same production entitlement service over HTTPS. The service compares a cryptographic digest but does not store or log the entered code. The app stores only the signed reviewer entitlement and cache time, and reviewer access expires after seven days. No study content or support correspondence is sent with these requests.

Knewly's entitlement database may store:

  • an encrypted purchase token and cryptographic token digests;
  • pseudonymous entitlement-chain and replay-prevention identifiers;
  • the product and base-plan identifiers;
  • subscription state, Premium status, expiration and grace-period dates;
  • verification, source-event, and update timestamps; and
  • limited message-processing records needed to prevent duplicate processing and reconcile subscription changes.

The service does not receive or store your name, contact details, Knewly study content, Knewly account information (because no Knewly account exists), advertising identifier, or payment-card details. It does receive the pseudonymous purchase and verification identifiers listed above. Infrastructure providers may process ordinary network and security data such as an IP address, request time, user-agent or protocol information, and abuse-prevention logs when requests reach their systems.

iOS and the App Store

Apple processes App Store purchases. Knewly uses StoreKit to request product information, start or restore a purchase, and read Apple's locally verified current entitlements. Knewly stores a minimal local Premium-status cache containing items such as product identifier, verification time, expiration, and any grace-period boundary. Transaction identifiers are used transiently to finish transactions and are not written to Knewly's entitlement cache. Knewly does not operate a separate iOS purchase-verification backend.

Purchase choices

Purchases are optional. You can manage or cancel a subscription through the app store that processed it. Removing Knewly does not automatically cancel a store subscription.

5. Support Communications

Knewly does not collect contact details during ordinary app use. If you choose to contact us at support@midnightmonstera.com, however, we receive the information you provide, such as your email address, any name associated with it, message, attachments, and diagnostic information. This support correspondence may identify you. Please do not send flashcard contents, personal images, purchase tokens, passwords, or other sensitive information unless it is necessary for us to help you.

We use support information to respond to you, troubleshoot problems, protect Knewly, and maintain appropriate business records. Your email provider and ours also process the communication under their own policies.

6. Information We Do Not Collect or Use

Knewly does not currently:

  • require or offer a Knewly user account;
  • ask for or maintain an identity profile containing your name, postal address, telephone number, birth date, login credentials, or government-issued identifier;
  • upload your deck names, card text, card images, pasted/imported study material, or study progress to Midnight Monstera;
  • include third-party behavioral analytics or crash-reporting SDKs;
  • include advertising or advertising SDKs;
  • use advertising identifiers;
  • sell personal information;
  • share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising or targeted advertising; or
  • use your study content to train artificial-intelligence models.

These statements concern Knewly's own practices. Your app store, operating system, browser, email provider, chosen sharing destination, or other third party may separately hold identity or payment information under its own privacy policy. Although we do not use the limited support, Premium, and network information described in Sections 4, 5, and 8 to identify you or build a profile, we treat it as personal information where applicable law does.

Knewly may produce limited on-device debug or startup diagnostics. Production logging is designed not to include deck names, card text, bulk-paste contents, or user images. Our entitlement service is configured not to log purchase tokens, request contents, or study content. It records only limited security diagnostics when an integrity check fails.

  • prevent replay, fraud, abuse, and unauthorized Premium activation;
  • respond to support requests;
  • diagnose failures and maintain the security and reliability of Knewly; and
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce applicable terms.

Where privacy law requires a legal basis, we generally rely on the following:

9. Retention

  • Local app data remains under your control as described in Section 2.
  • Exports and backups remain wherever you save or send them until you or the receiving service deletes them.
  • Android replay-prevention nonces expire after approximately 10 minutes. Unacknowledged Google Cloud Pub/Sub messages are configured for no more than 24 hours of retention; acknowledged messages are not retained by that subscription.
  • Android processed-notification records are deleted after 90 days. This includes completed, rejected, and stale processing records used to prevent duplicate notification handling.
  • Android entitlement records and encrypted purchase tokens are retained while a subscription may still require verification, restoration, reconciliation, refund, or voided-purchase handling. Entitlement and token-chain records are deleted once the entitlement's expiration date, final update, and any recorded billing-grace end date are all at least 24 months in the past, whatever its recorded state.
  • Knewly-controlled infrastructure logs are not maintained as a persistent application log archive during normal operation. If temporary logs are enabled for troubleshooting, they are deleted within 30 days. A limited record isolated for investigation of a confirmed or reasonably suspected security incident may be retained for up to 12 months, or longer if required by law. Infrastructure providers may retain their own security and audit logs under their published policies and contractual settings.
  • Support communications are deleted within 12 months after the final response or closure of the request. A specific communication may be retained longer when reasonably necessary for an unresolved dispute, security investigation, legal hold, or legal obligation.

When information is no longer reasonably necessary, we will delete or de-identify it, subject to technical limitations, security needs, backups, and legal requirements.

10. Security

Knewly is designed to minimize collection and keep study content local. We use measures appropriate to the nature of the information, including HTTPS for Android verification requests, cryptographic request binding and replay prevention, encrypted server-side storage of Android purchase tokens, signed entitlement responses, restricted service credentials, and app-store verification.

No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Device security, browser security, exported files, and destinations you select are also important. Use device access controls and keep backups in a location you trust.

11. Children and Families

Knewly is a general-audience study tool and is not directed to children under 13. Knewly does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. It requires no account, does not ask for a child's name, contact details, or birth date, has no advertising, and does not send study content to Midnight Monstera. Parents and guardians can use device and app-store parental controls to manage camera, photo, purchase, and sharing features.

If you believe a child under 13 has sent personal information to us, for example by email, contact support@midnightmonstera.com and we will delete it.

12. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

You can:

  • deny or revoke camera or photo permissions in device or browser settings;
  • edit or delete decks and cards in Knewly;
  • delete local Knewly data by using available app controls, clearing Knewly's browser site data, or uninstalling the app;
  • choose whether and where to export, back up, or share study data;
  • decline Knewly Premium and continue using available free features;
  • manage or cancel Premium through the applicable app store; and
  • choose whether to contact support or send a diagnostic report.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning personal information processed by Midnight Monstera, such as rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object, receive a portable copy, withdraw consent, or appeal a decision. You may also have the right to complain to a privacy or data-protection authority.

To make a request, email support@midnightmonstera.com with the subject "Privacy Request." We respond within 30 days, or sooner where the law requires. If we need more time where the law allows, we will tell you why. We may need enough information to understand and verify the request. Because Knewly has no user accounts, does not maintain identity profiles, and keeps most information on your device, we generally cannot identify you from local Knewly data.

Our entitlement records are not linked to your name, email address, or Google account, so we usually cannot identify which entitlement record belongs to you. When we cannot identify the relevant record, we cannot act on an access or deletion request for that record. It is deleted automatically under the retention schedule in Section 9. After a subscription ends, its record is deleted once its expiration date, last update, and any recorded billing-grace end date are all at least 24 months in the past. We will not ask you to send a raw purchase token by ordinary email.

Knewly does not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We therefore do not offer a sale/sharing opt-out. Knewly also does not use browser signals for targeted advertising. Where a legally binding browser-based opt-out signal applies to a practice we engage in, we will honor it as required.

Knewly does not track users across third-party websites or services, so it does not change its behavior in response to Do Not Track signals. We honor Global Privacy Control and similar signals where the law requires.
